### Overview/Comments

FANTASTIC Development tract on the Southbound side of I-45 AND FM 2004 very near 180,000 SF AMAZON DISTRIBUTION CENTER coming this year. Property has 3273' of frontage along SB I-45 and 2416' of frontage along FM-2004 per GCAD. No survey available.. Utilities are at the property and a possible TIRZ depending on the development. Property is just across FM 2004 from the current Greyhound Race Tract, just north of the recently announced 180,000 SF Amazon Distribution Center, just South of the New Lago Mar subdivision, Lago Mar Lagoon, Wal-Mart, Same, restaurants, banks, stores, Emmett Lowery Expressway, Mainland College, Mall of the Mainland, Worlds Gym, Altitudes Trampoline Park, etc. This is the perfect tract for an anchor with numerous pad sites. Heading South on I-45 take the FM-2004 exit and go thru the light and the property is at the corner of I-45 feeder and FM-2004. I-45 entrance in front of the property.
