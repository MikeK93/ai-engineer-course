Austintown Medical Center is a versatile medical office and outpatient-care facility located at 20 Ohltown Road in Austintown, Ohio. The 40,992 SF two story building sits on a 2.38-acre site. The most recent uses include urgent care, radiology, rehabilitation, orthopedics, otolaryngology, gastrointestinal, lab and general physician offices. The structure is primarily brick veneer construction with a multi-level structural system and EPDM low-slope roof.

## Highlights

- – Approximately 40,992 square feet of existing medical office/clinic space
- – Established healthcare-oriented building that is easily adaptable for multiple configurations
- – Originally constructed in 1980, with north-side additions completed in the early 2000’s
- – Two hydraulic passenger elevators, rated at 1,500 pounds and 4,500 pounds
- – Substantial utility and life-safety infrastructure in-place, including a 1,200-amp, 208/120-volt, 3-phase electrical service and a 150 kW diesel emergency generator
- – HVAC includes multiple split-system air-handling and condensing units; the building is heated by 10 gas-fired boilers
- – Long term management with institutional knowledge of the property and its systems
