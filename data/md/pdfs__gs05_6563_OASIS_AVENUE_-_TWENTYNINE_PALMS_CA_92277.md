## **<mark>HIGHLIGHTS</mark>**

###### **CALIFORNIA PROP 13**

California Real Estate investment is famous for Prop 13 which secures low property taxes assessed on initial purchase price. Property tax assessments only increase at 2% per year which has consistently been outpaced by property appreciation, rent growth and inflation over the last 50 years producing increased returns on an Year over Year basis for California Landlords unlike states like Texas that reassess property taxes on an annual basis at current market value.

###### **APPRECIATION**

California Real Estate is known for it’s strong appreciation to increase long-term hold returns.

###### **TAX BENEFITS**

California investors have the ability to depreciate the asset’s assessed improvement value over the course of 27.5 years producing an estimated $32,000 in annual tax depreciation write-off to hedge income.

###### **HEDGE AGAINST INFLATION**

In a time of record high inflation, the best hedge is Real Estate.

LOCATION OVERVIEW

The City of Twentynine Palms rests in the Mojave Desert of Southern California located in between Los Angeles and Phoenix, Arizona. The city is home to Joshua Tree National Park headquarters and north entrance, one of the most popular national parks in the United States. It is also the proud host of the Marine Corps Air Ground Combat Center, the world‘s largest Marine Corps training base. Locals of Southern California are familiar with Twentynine Palm’s proximity to the beautiful vacation town of Palm Springs and Indio, CA, the host of one of the World’s most famous music festivals, Coachella. The city offers a variety of amenities, including restaurants, hops, and parks. There are also a number of outdoor activities available, such as hiking, camping, rock climbing, and dirt biking. The city offers a variety of housing options, a mild climate, and easy access to outdoor recreation. Twentynine Palms has a mild climate year-round. The average temperature ranges from 65 degrees Fahrenheit in the winter to 100 degrees Fahrenheit in the summer.
