Hillside Rehabilitation Hospital is a purpose-built rehabilitation and healthcare campus totaling approximately 189,024 SF at 8747 Squires Lane in Warren, Ohio. The 21.06 acre campus includes six passenger elevators, including five hydraulic units and one traction unit, two dumbwaiters, emergency generators, a commercial kitchen, and a broad range of hospital-support infrastructure. Most recently occupied by Steward Healthcare (though December 2025) providing primarily inpatient rehabilitation services supported by radiology, sleep center, pharmacy (with compounding) and sleep center functions.

- – Significant power and life-safety infrastructure, including 2,000-amp, 120/208-volt, 3-phase electrical service, fire protection systems, a fire pump, emergency lighting, fire alarm system, and two diesel emergency generators

- – Potential applications may include rehabilitation, behavioral health, senior care, skilled nursing, medical office, specialty healthcare
