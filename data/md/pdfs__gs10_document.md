**03 ARCHITECTURE & OPERATING DNA**

## Superstructure with specialized DNA

The building only resembles concrete. Beneath painted stucco is a steel, wood, Glulam and shear-wall structure designed for longspan gallery use, heavy installations and controlled operations.

###### **Engineered superstructure**

###### **Glulam beams & shear walls**

Steel superstructure with aluminum and wood framing - a growthcapable shell intended for long-term use.

Long open spans, engineered strength and seismic load transfer to the foundation.

###### **Engineered hanging points**

###### **Gallery-grade art storage**

Approximately 12+ threaded-rod points, reportedly engineered for up to 5,000 lb each.

Rack coverage and protected back-of-house storage logic for significant works.

###### **Reinforced terrace**

###### **Climate-conscious service zones**
