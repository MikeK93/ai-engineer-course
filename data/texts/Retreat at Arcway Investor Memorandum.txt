INVESTMENT SUMMARY
Ashcroft Capital has identified Retreat at Arc Way (the “Property” or “Retreat”) as the first acquisition
within the Ashcroft Value-Add Fund II. Retreat at Arc Way is a 100% off-market transaction.
Ashcroft has acquired two properties (Macarthur Place & Estancia) from the same owner as Retreat
and has direct knowledge of both the operational and renovation upside opportunities that can be
capitalized on. Over the course of a 28 month hold period at Estancia, Ashcroft was able to increase
NOI by 75% and deliver a 34.1% IRR to investors. Additionally, Ashcroft has grown NOI at Macarthur
Place by 40% since acquisition in 2019, with substantial upside still remaining.
Retreat at Arc Way will mark Ashcroft’s 5th acquisition in Atlanta since entering the market in 2021.
Ashcroft is committed to scaling its Atlanta portfolio to take advantage of the rapid economic
expansion taking place across the entire MSA.
Retreat at Arc Way is a 284-unit property located in the Norcross submarket of Atlanta, GA.
Norcross is located in Gwinnett County and is regarded as one of the fastest growing submarkets in
Atlanta due to its close proximity to many Atlanta’s largest employment centers and top rated rated
school districts. The Property is located in the heart of Atlanta's I-85 industrial corridor, which is one
of the largest industrial hubs in the southeast and one of the main employment nodes in Atlanta.
Additionally, Norcross offers easy access to other major employment centers such as Peachtree
Corners, Sugarloaf, Buckhead, Sandy Springs, and Perimeter Center.
Norcross's multifamily market fundamentals continued to grow at historic rate in 2021. Rents
reached new record highs in both average asking and annual rent growth rates during 2021. Rents
in Norcross increased by 22% in 20211 (compared to the national average of 11.0%3) and the
average occupancy in the submarket has increased to an all time high of 96.8%1 With high
projected population growth, average household incomes exceeding $62,0001 in most parts of the
submarket, and other strong employment fundamentals, the Norcross multifamily market is
positioned to continue to grow for many years to come.
Retreat at Arc Way is a well-located property that offers significant upside through unit interior
renovations, amenity upgrades, and improving operational efficiency. The Property was built in
1986 and has an excellent mix of one, two, and three-bedroom units.
Current ownership has been primarily focused on maintaining high occupancy and addressing
major capital items over the last few years, inherently missing out on the rent growth momentum
the Norcross submarket has experienced. Ashcroft has the immediate opportunity to bring current
rents to market levels as well as renovate units to a high end renovation scope. Ashcroft has also
identified several additional revenue generating opportunities such as leasing washer/dryer sets to
residents and implementing a package locker fee.
Retreat at Arc Way
Having purchased two deals in the past from the seller, Ashcroft is very familiar with the value-add
and operational upside that they leave on the table. The current owner maintains a business plan
that focuses on low rents, high occupancy, and addressing major capital expenses. This leaves
Ashcroft with a great opportunity to focus on implementing a full scale interior value-add program
and improving operational efficiency.
Retreat at Arc Way is in great condition; However, thoughtful capital spending can significantly
improve the Property. The business plan includes several projects such as painting the Property and
improving the curb appeal. In order to fully reposition the Property, the amenities and common
areas are expected to be fully upgraded as well. The clubhouse, fitness center, pool areas, and other
amenities are expected to significantly be improved to further elevate the overall quality of the
Property.
The current owner has only renovated 27% of the units at the Property, which provides Ashcroft
the opportunity to bring the remaining 73% of units to a luxury level in order to be more
competitive with several renovated and newer properties in the area. Ashcroft plans on upgrading
the units to include wood-style flooring, quartz countertops with an undermount sink, stainless-steel
appliances, subway tile backsplash, painted cabinet fronts, updated fixtures & hardware, and a
modern lighting package. Renovated rents at the Property are projected to still be below the
immediate comp set leaving further room for upside. Additionally, Ashcroft plans on charging
residents for Washer/Dryers sets, adding a valet trash fee, and implementing a package locker fee.
All capital projects will be overseen by Ashcroft’s in-house construction team, Birchstone
Construction. Ashcroft’s in-house property management company, Birchstone Residential, will be
executing the business plan upon takeover.

BUSINESS PLAN
1. Enhanced Unit Renovation
i.
Unit interiors are inferior compared to surrounding competition. Ashcroft will renovate and modernize the property to be more competitive with
properties in the area by investing an average of approximately $8,800/unit on interior renovations over the next 24 months.
ii.
Rents at the Property are expected to increase by an average of approximately $479/mo as a result of both the renovations and increasing the
rents to current market levels.
2. Exterior and Common Area Capital Improvements
i.
Ashcroft plans to further elevate the quality of the Property by improving and modernizing the existing amenities to create a better living
experience for residents. All construction will be overseen by Ashcroft’s in-house team, Birchstone Construction.
ii.
The business plan at the Property includes: (i) repainting the property, (ii) updating the amenity and common areas,, (iii) addressing minor deferred
maintenance, and (iv) improving the curb appeal, landscaping, and signage.
iii.
Ashcroft anticipates reserving funds for Capital Improvements such as roof repairs and other deferred maintenance to preserve the Property.
3. Operational Improvement and Repositioning
i.
Upon the takeover of Retreat at Arc Way, the Partnership will retain Ashcroft’s in-house property management team, Birchstone Residential
(“Birchstone”), to manage the Property. Birchstone will bring a modern, best-in-class approach to tighten operations and improve resident
retention, which will increase performance of the asset throughout the hold period. Through a successful implementation of the business plan,
Retreat at Arc Way will be repositioned into one of the most desirable communities in the area.
ii.
Ashcroft and Birchstone have identified several areas to improve operations and increase revenue. Based on market research, it is the belief that
rents at the Property are significantly below market. Ashcroft and Birchstone plan to immediately bring rents to a market level in order to increase
income. Additionally, we will begin to charge residents for washer/dryer sets and implement a monthly package locker fee.
4. Private Loan Providing Maximum Flexibility
i.
Based on the projected cash flow, Ashcroft will pursue a 75% LTV initial loan from a private lender. The initial term will be 3 years with two one-year
extension options. This loan will provide investors with maximum flexibility by allowing for a capital event. Additionally, Ashcroft will be purchasing
an interest rate cap to further mitigate risk on increases in interest rates.
ii.
As NOI grows over year 1 and 2 through the repositioning of the asset, Ashcroft will explore refinancing with a longer term fixed-rate loan.
Although this has not been modeled into projections, Ashcroft plans to focus on a refinance that could return significant equity to the investors.
5. Exit Strategy
i.
Ashcroft will seek a disposition in approximately 5 years.

NORCROSS, GA
•
I-85 Industrial Corridor: One of the biggest employment hubs in Atlanta, the corridor contains over 150M
SF of industrial space ranging from large global distribution centers to e-commerce facilities. Companies
such as Amazon, Suzuki, Mitsubishi, Comcast, and HP all have a major presence in the corridor.
•
Gateway 85: The Gateway 85 Community Improvement District is the single largest of its kind in the state
of Georgia. Gateway 85 consists of more than 30 MSF of industrial, 5.7 MSF of retail space, 2.1 MSF of
office space, and supports more than 15,000 jobs.
•
Perimeter Center: Perimeter Center has 36 million SF of office space and is one of the largest employment
centers in the Southeast. This premier office submarket contains multiple global and regional headquarters
including Mercedes-Benz USA, UPS, Vertiv, and Westrock. Perimeter Center is also home to one of the most
comprehensive and concentrated medical facilities in the country nicknamed “Pill Hill”, which is made up of
three major hospitals and hundreds of medical practices.
•
Peachtree Corners: Peachtree Corners is Gwinnett County’s newest city, incorporated in 2005 and
founded in 2012. Peachtree Corners now boasts over 43,000 residents and is over 17 square miles. Some of
the major employers that have a presence here are FedEx, Siemens, and Immucor. Additionally, Peachtree
Corners is home to The Curiosity Lab, a 500-acre technology park that features a 5G-enabled 3-mile
autonomous vehicle test track and smart city lab.
Major Demand Drivers
Home to one of the metro's larger industrial nodes, Norcross has become a top distribution and manufacturing
hub in Atlanta.1 Several major corporations maintain headquarters or regional operations in the submarket,
helping drive strong demand for multifamily product. Mitsubishi Electric, HP (Hewlett-Packard), Comcast, Asbury
Automotive, and agricultural equipment manufacturer AGCO Corporation all have global headquarters in
Norcross. Additionally, over the last few years, Amazon has opened several state-of-the-art distribution and Prime
Now hubs in the submarket creating thousands of new high paying jobs.
Norcross is located less than 5-minutes from I-285 & I-85 which offer residents easy access to other major centers
of employment and entertainment such as Sandy Springs, Peachtree Corners, Buckhead, and Perimeter Center.

Coupon Amounts:
Two-Tiered Return Structure: Investors have the opportunity to invest in Class A and/or Class B
Limited Partnership Interests.
Limited Partner (A) - Class A: Class A Limited Partner’s earn a coupon of 9% per annum of such
Limited Partner’s investment in the Partnership (the “Class A Coupon”). Class A Limited Partners
have limited distributions upon disposition of the Property. This tier offers stronger projected
cash flow and reduced risk as compared to Class B Limited Partners.
Limited Partner (B) – Class B: Class B Limited Partners earn a coupon of 7% per annum of such
Limited Partner’s investment in the Partnership (the “Class B Coupon”). Upon the disposition of
the Property, after payment of debt, return of Class A and Class B Limited Partner investments,
payment of any unpaid Class A and Class B Coupon amounts, and then, pro rata, seventy percent
(70%) to the Class B Limited Partners and thirty percent (30%) to the General Partner until such
time as the Class B Limited Partners have received a cumulative amount equal to thirteen percent
(13%) IRR. Then, Class B Limited Partners will receive 50% of the remaining proceeds from
disposition. This tier has a lower coupon but provides greater participation upon disposition or
capital event compared Class A Limited Partners.
