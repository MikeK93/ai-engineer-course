PROPERTY OVERVIEW
Newmark is pleased to present 8000 Wisconsin Avenue, a fully entitled, 
residential mixed-use development opportunity in the heart of the 
Woodmont Triangle in Bethesda, Maryland. The 20-story building has been 
designed as a state-of-the art apartment complex with 437 units ranging 
from studios to 3-bedroom floor plans as well as 19,500 square feet of 
Class-A retail space. 
The property consists of an assemblage of parcels with frontage on both 
Wisconsin Avenue and Woodmont Avenue. Designed to maximize light and 
architectural interest, the building features an interior courtyard, large multi-
level atrium and rooftop amenities. Abundant on-site parking is provided in 
a multi-level below grade garage.
Future residents will enjoy outstanding, immediate access to all of the 
upscale amenities the area has to offer. From fine dining to exclusive retailers, 
downtown Bethesda has no shortage of destinations for all to enjoy. The 
property is steps away from the new Marriott International headquarters as 
well as the Bethesda Station Metro Stop (WMATA Red Line). 
LOCATION
The property is located on the west side of Wisconsin Avenue, just south 
of Cordell Avenue in the Woodmont Triangle neighborhood of Bethesda 
Maryland. With frontage and access on both Wisconsin Avenue and 
Woodmont Avenue, the location and convenience of this site is unparalleled. 
Transportation alternatives include immediate access to the Bethesda Metro 
Station (WMATA Red Line), Metro Bus service along Wisconsin Avenue and 
a planned Bus Rapid Transit Station in front of the property. The option 
for multiple commuting routes into DC or other parts of the region further 
enhances the location’s convenience and accessibility. Shopping and 
services are within an easy walk including a Harris Teeter Grocery Store two 
blocks north of the site. Wisconsin Avenue is a strong employment corridor 
highlighted by the new Marriott Headquarters just three blocks south of 
the site.

BETHESDA, MARYLAND
Located directly north of Washington, DC, the Bethesda submarket is a prestigious 
mixed-use community that offers an attractive suburban setting within a vibrant 
urban environment. The area is highly desirable from a residential perspective 
given the wide array of diverse lifestyle amenities, the thriving business hub and 
convenient public transit accessibility.
Downtown Bethesda boasts a healthy corporate environment with many large-
scale office users securing new long-term leases including Fox/ WTTG, ProShares 
and JBG Smith. Also, in a clear testament to its commitment to Bethesda, 
Marriott International recently announced plans to relocate its 3,500-Employee 
headquarters to a brand new $600 million complex in Bethesda’s downtown 
district. The project, which will also include a 244-room Marriott hotel, broke 
ground in mid-2018 and is slated for delivery in 2022. Another Fortune 500 
company, defense giant Lockheed Martin, has a Bethesda address in its complex 
to the north off of I-270 on Rockledge Drive.
Comprising 10.9 million square feet of space, the Bethesda office market 
boasts the highest asking rental rate in suburban Maryland- nearly $45/SF. The 
submarket continues to expand, featuring nearly 1.4 million SF of office space 
currently under construction.
With the Metro station just steps away from the property, residents will enjoy 
the benefit of seamless public transit access to Washington, DC and throughout 
the surrounding region via the Metro’s Red Line. Each weekday, an average of 
approximately 9,100 passengers board the Bethesda Metro.
Downtown Bethesda will also be a bookend to the Purple Line, a 16-mile, $5.6 
billion, East-West line, connecting Bethesda to New Carrollton in Prince George’s 
County. Many stops will connect riders with Metro as well as MARC commuter 
rail. The project is under construction and planned to open in 2022.
The area is a vibrant urban center that serves as home to a dynamic mix of 
restaurants, shops and service retailers, art galleries and live performance theaters. 
The area has been designated as an official Arts & Entertainment District by the 
state of Maryland, facilitating a high quality of life for local residents. Downtown 
Bethesda is poised for further development via the Bethesda Downtown Plan, 
which was adopted in May 2017 by the Montgomery County Council. The plan 
aims to build on downtown Bethesda’s success by increasing its mixed-use 
density, enhancing its parks/open spaces and improving its walkability to ensure 
its long-term sustainability.

CURRENT OFFICE PROJECTS 
MARRIOTT HEADQUARTERS – 7750 WISCONSIN AVENUE
	 Developer: The Bernstein Companies/Boston Properties
	 Status: Under Construction
	 Size: 795,500 SF
The largest and most prominent office project coming to Bethesda 
will be occupied by a sole tenant — Marriott International. The 
building has progressed through site prep., demolition, and 
excavation phase, and is currently undergoing vertical construction. 
The project is being developed by a joint venture of Bernstein and 
Boston Properties which will own and lease the 22-story office 
building to Marriott. A 13-story, 230-room Flagship Marriott hotel is 
also going up on the site, with a public plaza located between the 
two buildings. The office building is still on schedule to deliver in Q2 
2021, with the hotel opening in Q1 2022.
THE WILSON – 7272 WISCONSIN AVENUE
	 Developer: Carr Properties
	 Status: Under Construction
	 Size: 360,800 SF
The Wilson broke ground in late 2017 and recently topped out. The 
majority of its space has been leased ahead of delivery later this 
year. The 24-story building shares a parking garage podium with 
the Elm apartment building and is now the tallest office building 
in Montgomery County. It also sits directly on top of the future 
Bethesda Purple Line station, as well as a new southern entrance to 
the existing Red Line Metro station.
4747 BETHESDA AVENUE
	 Developer: JBG Smith
	 Status: Delivered
	 Size: 280,000 SF
The first in the current generation of trophy office product in 
Bethesda, 4747 Bethesda delivered late last year, nearly fully leased. 
The trophy office building is located on the same block as the Wilson 
and immediately across from the Bethesda Row retail district. The 
building’s developer, JBG Smith, is also its largest tenant, occupying 
about 80,000 SF. Host Hotels, Booz Allen Hamilton, Orano USA, and 
Pebblebrook REIT round out the tenant roster.

	 Developer: Stonebridge Associates

Like the Marriott headquarters, the 23-story 7373 Wisconsin Ave.

BETHESDA BIO – 8280 WISCONSIN AVENUE
	 Developer: Donohoe/Stonebridge Associates
	 Status: Planned (Groundbreaking possibly by end of 2020)
	 Size: 175,000 SF
The Bethesda Bio building is unique among the submarket’s office 
pipeline as a purpose-built lab/office building targeted at life sciences 
tenants. The building would be the first of its kind in Bethesda, and the 
first in an urban location in Montgomery County. While the county is 
home to one of the largest life sciences clusters in the nation, nearly 
the entire lab inventory is located along the I-270 Corridor in Rockville, 
Gaithersburg, and Germantown in suburban-style campuses. The 
only notable exception is the massive United Therapeutics campus in 
downtown Silver Spring. Bethesda Bio provides a unique opportunity 
for biotech, pharmaceutical, and other research firms to locate in an 
urban, transit-accessible environment, less than a mile from NIH which 
is the region’s largest single source of funding for research.

WASHINGTON DC MSA
The Washington, DC metropolitan area is comprised of the nation’s capital, Northern 
Virginia, and suburban Maryland and is home to more than 6.2 million people, making it the 
sixth largest metropolitan area in the country. Due to its diverse employment opportunities, 
excellent transportation infrastructure, and vast cultural resources, the region has continually 
attracted a highly educated and affluent workforce, predominantly made up of people 
between the ages of 25 and 34—the prime renter demographic.
The Washington DC Metro region, consisting of Suburban Maryland, Northern Virginia, and 
the District of Columbia, continues to perform as one of the strongest and most resilient 
economies in the United States. Over the past 25 years, the Washington DC Metro Area has 
only experienced one year of negative job growth and has been considered the safest place 
to invest among the major metropolitan areas during times of market volatility.
During previous economic downturns, the Washington DC Metro generally has outperformed 
its peer cities—due in large part to the presence of the federal government and its spending, 
of which, the large majority is captured by local contractors. The Washington DC Metro has 
been a magnet for capital during recessionary trends and economic uncertainty due to its 
stable economic performance. During the Great Recession, the Washington DC Metro saw 
a shallower decline than peer markets, and it saw the strongest growth as the United States 
began to recover in 2010. This led to Washington DC’s office market achieving significantly 
higher cumulative office market returns during this period.
The Washington DC Metro region is home to the largest and fastest growing data center 
market in the world, and it is home to the largest number of cybersecurity firms in the United 
States, creating an attractive platform for continued growth in the constantly evolving 
technology sector. 
Companies including Amazon, Google, Facebook, Microsoft, and Apple, among many 
others, have large presence in the area and continue to attract a highly educated workforce 
predominantly made up of individuals between the ages of 25 and 34, a prime renter 
demographic. Amazon, in particular, is now one of the leading drivers of the area economy 
with its selection of National Landing as its new East Coast Headquarters in Cyrstal City, less 
than 11 miles from the Bethesda and accessible via Metro.

THE OWNER WILL CONSIDER THE FOLLOWING 
SALE OR FINANCING STRUCTURES FOR THE 
PROPERTY:
1)	 Completed Building Presale. The Seller will develop 
the project and sell to a buyer at a pre-negotiated price. 
Closing will occur at the time of building completion or 
upon stabilization. The project will be delivered based 
upon pre-approved design and finish specifications 
using an agreed upon general contractor. 
2)	 Land Sale-Ground Lease. The Seller will sell the 
entitled land, develop the project and enter into a long- 
term ground lease with the new land owner. Under this 
scenario the land purchaser may provide additional 
equity as part of the project financing plan. 
3)	 Joint Venture. The Seller will contribute the land into 
a joint venture with a partner who will contribute 
additional equity required for project completion. 
4)	 Land Sale. The Seller will sell the land with all 
entitlements in place, but have no ongoing involvement 
with construction or lease up post closing.
