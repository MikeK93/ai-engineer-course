Experience Parkshore - a connected, four-building campus where nature, energy, and convenience come together. Ideally located outside Sacramento, and nestled in the nearby foothills of Folsom, Parkshore offers modern spaces, on-site amenities, freeway connectivity, and nearby trails, all within a campus-style environment that supports today’s dynamic workforce.

Highlights

  • 4-building, multi-tenant campus environment
  • Flexible, plug-and-play office spaces
  • On-site amenities: tenant lounge, fitness center, conference center and training room
  • Central location with immediate access to Highway 50
  • Adjacent to Out of Bounds Brewery, and 5-minute drive to retail, dining, and entertainment options on Historic Sutter Street
  • Direct access to nearby Humbug Willow Creek and American River trails
