Industrial building in Chandler, Arizona, prominently located at 6900 W. Morelos Place with excellent access to Loop 202 and I-10.

6900 W. Morelos Place is a well-positioned 58,699 SF industrial building in Chandler, Arizona, offering a compelling opportunity for an owner-user or investor seeking a functional industrial asset. Built in 2002, the property features modern construction and a substantial footprint well suited for a range of industrial operations. Its prominent location provides excellent access to Loop 202 and I-10, supporting efficient regional connectivity. This property presents a rare chance to acquire a sizable industrial facility in a highly desirable South Phoenix submarket with strong fundamentals and lasting utility.
